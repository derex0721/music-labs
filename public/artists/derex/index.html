李泊崴derex

流行Pop異國Global influences民謠Folk

嗨，我是李泊崴，也可以叫我泊崴或崴哥。

八年前，一堂創作課讓我寫下了第一首原創歌曲，也開啟了我的音樂創作之旅。我擅長作曲，喜歡流行、異國與民謠曲風，持續在不同的音樂風景裡尋找靈感。

希望你能在我的作品裡，找到喜歡的旋律。

Hi, I'm derex (李泊崴). You can also call me Bo-Wei or Wei-Ge.

Eight years ago, a songwriting course led me to write my first original song—and that was the start of my creative journey. Composing is my strength, and I'm drawn to pop, folk, and sounds from different cultures, always finding inspiration along the way.

I hope you'll find a melody you love in my music.
